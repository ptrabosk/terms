Offsight Timer Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Offsight Timer is a browser extension that helps authorized users record time spent on work items. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how that information is used.

Information we handle

Offsight Timer may handle:

  • a browser profile email and work account identifier;
  • work-item and company identifiers;
  • work-page URLs and related page context;
  • timer start and stop times, durations, idle time, and prompt responses;
  • work-item status, assignment, availability, and time-entry information; and
  • limited authentication context needed to submit time to a supported work platform.

The extension does not collect browser passwords or Google OAuth access tokens. Raw page content and authentication credentials are not included in completed timer records sent to the timer service.

How we use information

Information is used only to:

  • confirm that a user is authorized to use the extension;
  • operate, display, stop, and recover timers;
  • calculate and submit work-time records;
  • store completed timer sessions;
  • prevent duplicate processing and retry failed operations; and
  • maintain security and troubleshoot operational problems.

Storage and transmission

Active timer state and retry information may be stored locally in browser extension storage. Completed timer records are transmitted over HTTPS to company-controlled services and stored in an operational database. Time entries may also be submitted to a supported work platform through the user's authenticated browser session.

Sharing

We do not sell timer data. We do not use timer data for advertising, lending, or credit decisions. Information is shared only when needed to operate the extension and supported services, maintain security, comply with law, or complete an approved corporate transaction.

Retention

Records are retained according to applicable workforce, operational, security, and legal requirements. Pending or failed operations may be retained until they are resolved.

Security

Personal and sensitive information is transmitted using HTTPS. Access to operational systems is restricted through company access controls. All executable extension code is included in the extension package; the extension does not download or execute remote code.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information obtained through Chrome permissions is used only to provide or improve the extension's user-facing timer functionality.

Your choices and requests

Authorized users may contact their administrator or internal Privacy, Security, or IT support channel to ask questions or request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable policy and legal retention requirements.
