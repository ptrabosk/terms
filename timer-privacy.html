How Zendesk Timer handles data

Effective September 24, 2026

Zendesk Timer is an internal workforce extension for authorized Attentive personnel.

← Return home

Purpose and scope

Zendesk Timer tracks time spent working a single Zendesk ticket across Zendesk and Attentive, writes calculated Effort to Zendesk, and sends completed timer-session records to an Attentive-controlled operational service. This policy describes the data handled by the Chrome extension and its companion timer service.

Information handled

The extension handles the following information when needed for its single purpose:

  • the primary Chrome profile email, used only to confirm that it belongs to attentivemobile.com or concierge.attentivemobile.com;
  • the agent's Zendesk user ID;
  • Zendesk ticket IDs, company IDs, ticket status, assignment, availability signals, and the configured Effort field value;
  • Zendesk and Attentive work URLs associated with a timer session;
  • timer start and stop times, work-domain duration, idle duration, prompt decisions, and diagnostic event timing;
  • Zendesk form fields, visible ticket text, and ticket comments only as needed to identify structured timer context such as a company ID; and
  • session-scoped Zendesk authentication context, including a CSRF token, when needed to perform direct Effort writeback.

Raw ticket-comment text and general visible page text are not retained or uploaded as timer-session records. The Zendesk CSRF token and browser-session credentials are not sent to the timer API. The extension does not collect a Google password or Google OAuth access token.

How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • authorize workforce access to timer functionality;
  • start, display, stop, and recover one active ticket timer;
  • attribute work time between Zendesk, Attentive, idle time, and other activity;
  • calculate and write Zendesk Effort;
  • store completed operational timer records;
  • prevent duplicate session processing and safely retry failed operations; and
  • investigate and resolve operational failures.

Storage and transmission

Active timer state, ticket context, authentication context needed for writeback, and retry information may be stored in Chrome extension storage on the user's device. Completed timer sessions are transmitted over HTTPS to the Attentive timer API and stored in its associated operational database.

Uploaded session records may include the agent email and Zendesk user ID, ticket and company IDs, work URLs, start and stop timestamps, work and idle durations, structured URL and network-event metadata, stop reason, prompt-related timing, and Zendesk writeback result. Request and response bodies observed from Zendesk or Attentive are not included as timer-session data.

Zendesk Effort is currently read and written directly through the agent's authenticated Zendesk browser session. The browser may attach its Zendesk cookies to those Zendesk requests; the extension does not upload the cookie values to the timer API.

Retention and user requests

Local and server records are retained according to Attentive's applicable workforce, operational, security, and legal retention requirements. Pending, failed, or ambiguous operations may be retained until reconciled. Authorized users may request access, correction, or deletion through their Attentive administrator or the applicable internal Privacy, Security, or IT support channel, subject to company policy and legal retention obligations.

Sharing and prohibited uses

Timer data is not sold, used for personalized advertising, or used for lending or credit decisions. It is transferred only when necessary to operate Zendesk and Attentive services, protect security, comply with law, or complete an approved corporate transaction.

Human access is limited to circumstances allowed by the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including explicit authorization or consent, security purposes, legal obligations, and permitted aggregated or de-identified internal operations.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information obtained through Chrome permissions is used only to provide or improve the extension's single user-facing purpose.

Security

Personal and sensitive information is transmitted using HTTPS. Access to operational systems is restricted through company access controls. The extension requests only the Chrome and host permissions needed for timer operation, recovery, profile authorization, scoped prompts, upload, and Zendesk writeback.

All executable extension code is included in the distributed extension package. The extension does not download or execute remotely hosted code.

Questions and requests

Authorized users should contact their Attentive administrator or the applicable internal Privacy, Security, or IT support channel with questions or requests concerning this policy or their timer data.
