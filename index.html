Attentive workforce tool

One ticket.

Zendesk Timer tracks time spent on one Zendesk ticket while an authorized agent works across Zendesk and Attentive.

Read the privacy policy How it works

Single purpose

Accurate work-time records

01

Authorized access

New timers require the primary Chrome profile to use an approved Attentive email domain. No Google password or OAuth access token is collected.

02

Work-domain timing

The extension attributes active work on Zendesk and Attentive while keeping idle and unrelated browsing separate from calculated Effort.

03

Operational records

Completed sessions are sent securely to an Attentive-controlled timer service, and calculated Effort is written to the associated Zendesk ticket.

Privacy by design

Clear about the data required

The extension handles identity, ticket context, work URLs, timing, and prompt decisions only to provide its timer, recovery, Zendesk writeback, and operational record features.

It does not sell timer data or use it for advertising or lending decisions.
